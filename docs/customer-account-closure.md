# Customer account closure

Closure is the reversible access-ending operation for an individual customer. It revokes sessions, marks the user and owned individual account closed, cancels active/pending commercial subscriptions, suspends active licenses, and deactivates devices. It blocks login/session creation, checkout, renewal, trial, download, signed grant use, license reveal, and activation.

Closure does not delete commerce, invoices, payments, refunds, licenses, webhook records, audit/security evidence, email evidence, or legal acceptances. Reopen is explicit, recent-admin protected, and audited. It restores account/login state only; entitlements remain suspended for deliberate review.

An owner of an organization cannot use individual closure until ownership is transferred or a future organization-closure workflow is approved. Membership in unrelated organizations is not removed when an individual account closes.
